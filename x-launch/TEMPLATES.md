# The 30 templates

Texts and sheets to copy, then fill in. Fields to fill are written `{{field}}`. The messages read like a note to a friend in the same line of work: short, lowercase if you like, one ask at a time. Make them sound like you before you send them.

---

<a id="launch-log"></a>
## The launch log

[Step 02](STEPS.md#step-02) · One row per launch in a spreadsheet, so you know which angle paid off. Paste this line as the sheet's header.

```
date,angle,account,post_url,creators,creator_budget,views_48h,likes,bookmarks,replies,quotes,visits,signups,paid,new_mrr,cost_per_signup,notes
```

<a id="countdown-48h"></a>
## The 48h countdown

[Step 06](STEPS.md#step-06) · What must be done at each checkpoint, counted back from T0, the minute the post goes out.

```
T−48h   date, minute, time zone and owner set
T−30h   brief written, angle picked, posting account picked
T−24h   final post; creator shortlist sent
T−18h   prices agreed; creator angles and visuals ready; team and newsletter lined up
T−12h   creators confirmed, money set aside; reply plan; GO or NO-GO
T−3h    count of confirmations
T0      post published, pinned, creators pinged
T+5m    first wave of quotes
T+1h    everyone has quoted
T+2h    founder still in the replies
T+1d    creator posts checked
T+2d    people who engaged turned into a lead list
T+3d    retro in numbers, next slot booked
```

<a id="the-brief"></a>
## The brief

[Step 07](STEPS.md#step-07) · The single page the team, the agents and later the creator kit all start from. No empty line: "unknown" becomes an open question with a name on it.

```
WIN PLAN         {{the result you want, the lever that gets it, the number}}
WHAT             {{what you are launching, in one sentence}}
KPI              {{one metric and its target}}
TIME             {{date, minute, time zone, in the exact words of whoever posts}}
POST FROM        {{the account that posts}}
LINK             {{the one link, visible without "Show more"}}
BUDGET           {{what goes to creators}}
PROMISED         {{what we promised people}}
OPEN QUESTIONS   {{what is still missing, and who to ask}}
RISKS            {{what could sink the whole thing}}
NOT THIS TIME    {{what we keep for the next launch}}
```

<a id="two-minute-check"></a>
## The two-minute check

[Step 14](STEPS.md#step-14) · Run it on every creator before you book them. Two minutes per account.

```
[ ] read 10 recent posts (not the pinned one)
[ ] replies come from real accounts, from the right crowd
[ ] views per post fit the follower count
[ ] they talk about the topic without being paid to
[ ] last paid post: over a week ago, and it did not flop
[ ] nothing in the last month you would not want next to your name
Verdict: yes / maybe / no, plus one line on why
```

## The invite DM

[Step 16](STEPS.md#step-16) · Your first message to a creator. You ask for their price, you don't give yours.

```
hey {{first_name}}! it's {{your_first_name}} from {{product}}
we're launching {{what, in one line}} on {{day}} and i'd love to have you in on it
it goes out at {{time}}, {{time_zone}}. are you around?
if so, what do you charge for a quote post? i'll send the brief and access so you can try it first
```

## The angle sheet

[Step 17](STEPS.md#step-17) · Two or three angles spun off the main one, each for one audience, and who carries it.

```
MAIN POST   {{angle}}
ANGLE A, for {{audience}}   {{the idea}}   who: {{accounts}}
ANGLE B, for {{audience}}   {{the idea}}   who: {{accounts}}

Avoid: the main post's wording, other creators' wording, and anything the product can't do.
```

<a id="creator-folder"></a>
## The creator folder

[Step 18](STEPS.md#step-18) · One shared folder where each creator finds everything they need.


<a id="creator-kit"></a>
## The kit sent to each creator

[Step 19](STEPS.md#step-19) · One message with the angle, the draft, the visuals and the launch-day steps. You wait for a yes.

```
hey {{first_name}}! here's everything for the {{product}} launch. it goes out on {{day}} at {{time}}, {{time_zone}}
your angle: {{angle}}
a draft to get you started: {{draft}}
rewrite it however you like, or pitch your own angle, happy to look
clips, screenshots and access: {{folder_link}}
i'll ping you the second the post is live. on the day:
1. go to @{{account}} and open the pinned post
2. like and bookmark
3. reply
4. post your quote
5. plain repost 10 min later
if you can hang around the first hour and reply to a few other creators, that would be great
you in?
```

<a id="team-note"></a>
## The team note

[Step 23](STEPS.md#step-23) · So every teammate has their quote ready and their first hour blocked.

```
team, we're launching {{what}} on {{day}} at {{time}}, {{time_zone}}. the post goes out from @{{account}}
before: write your quote today and send it to me. your words, about your part of the work
on the day i'll say "we're live" here. go to the pinned post on the profile, like and bookmark, reply, then post your quote
first hour: everyone in the replies. {{first_name}} takes tech questions, {{first_name}} takes pricing questions, and everyone says thanks
two things to avoid: using the same words as everyone else, and posting before the main post is live
thanks, this hour really matters
```

<a id="good-reply"></a>
## A reply that works

[Step 26](STEPS.md#step-26) · The shape of a reply in someone else's thread.

```
a real point on what the post says: agree and take it further, or disagree with a reason
one line of something you saw or built that backs it up
optional: "we shared the numbers today" + link, only if it answers the post
```

<a id="eight-gates"></a>
## The eight GO / NO-GO gates

[Step 29](STEPS.md#step-29) · At T−12h, each gate is a yes or a no. A yes without proof counts as a no.

```
G1  Creator money set aside                                  yes / no
G2  Final post approved and frozen                           yes / no
G3  Creators confirmed in writing: {{n}} (30 or more)        yes / no
G4  Every creator has their angle, draft and visuals         yes / no
G5  Network warned, team briefed                             yes / no
G6  Link visible without "Show more", and it opens           yes / no
G7  Reply plan written, first replies drafted                yes / no
G8  Expected numbers written down                            yes / no
Verdict: GO / GO with risks ({{which}}) / NO-GO ({{blocker}}, {{owner}}, {{deadline}})
```

[Step 32](STEPS.md#step-32) · One message, at the same second for everyone, no link.

<a id="three-replies"></a>
## Three kinds of replies

[Step 35](STEPS.md#step-35) · How to answer what shows up under the post.

```
The fan ("this is great")                   ask what they'd use it for, then answer that
The question ("does it do X?")              one line of answer, then show it: a clip or a screenshot
The skeptic ("isn't this just Y?")          agree on what's true, then name the one difference that matters. never twice
```
